# PrivAI-RCPS 2027: website and hosting guide

## Preview on your computer
Unzip the package and double-click index.html. It opens in your browser. No installation is required.

## Give the site to your university or ACNS
Send index.html and the assets folder to the person managing their web server. Ask them to upload both together into a workshop directory, such as /workshops/privai-rcps-2027/, and enable HTTPS. The page works at a domain root or within a subdirectory. It contains its own styles, bundles its fonts and campus photograph locally, requires no database and has no build step. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.

If ACNS only accepts content within its existing website system, they may need to import the text rather than upload the complete page. Confirm their hosting arrangements with the workshop chair.

## Optional independent hosting
The same index.html can be used as the homepage of a GitHub Pages repository. This package does not create a GitHub account or repository.

## Update the page
Open index.html in a text editor (for example VS Code), change the text, save, and upload the revised file. Keep a backup first. You can also ask ChatGPT to revise the page for you.

## Details to confirm before public launch
- Workshop acceptance and final description
- Submission and notification deadlines, including time zones
- Exact workshop date and venue
- Paper template, page limits, submission categories and review anonymity
- Submission portal URL
- Workshop contact email and chair profile links
- Programme committee membership and consent to list members
- Keynotes and final programme

The page uses the supplied proposal for its scope, topics, chairs and tentative agenda. Suggested submission and notification dates and the proceedings deadline come from the ACNS 2027 Call for Workshops (https://acns2027.isg.rhul.ac.uk/calls/workshops/). No internal proposal PDF, expected acceptance statistics or chair biographies beyond brief research interests are included.

The public website and CFP are due 12 November 2026 AoE according to the ACNS call. The private review site is separate from your eventual university or ACNS hosting.
